\begin{recipe}{Quick Refried Beans}{\textit{Serves: 2}}{\textit{Preparation Time: 15 minutes}}
    \freeform
    I've made these beans hundreds of times over the years to serve with any variety of Mexican-ish meals. They will suffice as a side dish, a dip, or even a filling for burritos and quesadillas.

    \Ingredient{1 Tbsp neutral oil}

    Preheat a medium sauce pan or skillet on medium heat. Add oil.

    \Ingredient{1 can pinto or black beans, drained \& rinsed}
    \Ingredient{\fr{1}{2} cup stock}
    \Ingredient{\fr{1}{4} cup sour cream}
    \Ingredient{2 tsp galic powder}
    \Ingredient{2 tsp onion powder}
    \Ingredient{black pepper, to taste}
    \Ingredient{salt, to taste}

    Add all ingredients to food processor and blend until beans are nearly fully broken down.

    \Ingredient{Dash of chili powder}
    \Ingredient{Dash of cumin}

    Add chili powder and cumin to hot oil to bloom. Quickly add mixture from food processor to pan and stir. Simmer on low for 5-10 minutes, stirring frequently, until beans are heated through and have reached desired consistency. Add additional stock if you want them thinner. The beans will thicken as they cool, so if you are serving them later, you may want to add a little more stock to account for this.
\end{recipe}
